PREPIFY: interview experiences, questions and answers for any company
=====================================================================

START
1. Unzip this folder anywhere (for example, your Desktop).
2. Double-click Prepify.exe.
   - If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click "More info", then "Run anyway".
     (The app isn't code-signed, so Windows warns about it.)
3. A black window opens and Prepify opens in your browser at http://localhost:5000.
   Keep the black window open while you use Prepify. Close it to stop Prepify.

SEARCH
Type a company and role, for example "Accenture data analyst interview questions".
Without signing in you get links to every source.

PULL QUESTIONS AND EXPERIENCES INTO PREPIFY
1. Click "Sign in" (top right) and sign in with your Google account.
2. Add the Prepify Capture extension to Chrome (one time):
   a. Open chrome://extensions in Chrome.
   b. Turn on "Developer mode" (top right).
   c. Click "Load unpacked" and choose the "extension" folder from this zip.
   d. Reload the Prepify tab.
3. Search a company and click "Pull all sources". Prepify reads the sites in the
   background with your own Chrome. If a site needs you to sign in, Prepify tells you.

AI ASSIST (OPTIONAL)
AI assist tidies pulled questions, reads job descriptions in more depth and understands
unusual searches. It needs your own free Groq key:
1. Create a key at https://console.groq.com/keys
2. In this folder, next to Prepify.exe, create a text file named .env containing one line:
   GROQ_API_KEY=your-key-here
3. Close the black window and start Prepify.exe again. Then turn on AI assist in the side panel.

PRIVACY
Everything you pull stays in your browser tab and is erased when you close the tab or sign out.
Prepify keeps no database. Your Google, Glassdoor and other passwords are never seen by Prepify.

PROBLEMS
- "Port 5000 is being used by another program": close that program and start Prepify again.
- Google sign-in says access is blocked: ask the person who shared Prepify to add your Google
  account as a test user.
